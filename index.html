01 · Problem

Mobile robots need to recognize the conditions of their surroundings in order to move autonomously. An obstacle in their path can prevent forward movement and cause a collision.

This project aims to enable the JetBot to visually identify free and obstructed spaces, allowing it to respond to obstacles through evasive maneuvers.

02 · Objective

To develop a navigation system for the JetBot using a neural network trained with images of its surroundings, capable of classifying path conditions and supporting the robot's decision-making process.

04 · Dataset

A dataset of 200 images was created, divided into two categories to train the neural network.

100 Free Spaces
100 Obstructed Spaces

Free spaces: images showing paths without obstacles.

Obstructed spaces: images showing wall edges and chair legs, located at a reported distance of between 5 and 10 mm.

50% Free
50% Obstructed

05 · Work Methodology

The development process was organized into four main stages:

01 · DATA COLLECTION Collection of 200 images from the environment.
02 · DATASET Classification into free and obstructed spaces.
03 · TRAINING Pattern learning through the neural network.
04 · NAVIGATION Detection tests and obstacle avoidance maneuvers.

06 · Neural Networks

An artificial neural network is a mathematical model inspired by the way the human brain works, designed to learn patterns from data.

  • Input: receives the image pixels.
  • Hidden layers: process features such as edges and shapes.
  • Output: generates a prediction about the image class.

During training, weights and biases are adjusted to reduce errors through a process known as backpropagation.

07 · RGB and BGR

Digital images are made up of pixels represented through three color channels.

RGB: organizes the channels in the order Red, Green, and Blue.

BGR: uses the order Blue, Green, and Red, commonly used in OpenCV.

Each channel has values ranging from 0 to 255, representing the intensity of each color.

BGR · B G R

08 · OpenCV Library

OpenCV is a computer vision library that allows images and videos to be processed and analyzed.

It provides tools for loading images, resizing them, changing color spaces, and processing video frames.

In robotics, it facilitates the processing of visual information used by the system to interpret its surroundings.

09 · Markers

In mobile robotics, markers are visual references that help robots recognize locations, determine their orientation, or perform specific tasks.

Natural markers: elements already present in the environment, such as corners, textures, doors, and floor patterns.

Artificial markers: references intentionally placed in the environment, such as QR codes, visual labels, and geometric patterns.

11 · Conclusion

This project integrates computer vision, machine learning, and mobile robotics into an image-classification-based navigation system. Through a balanced dataset, the JetBot can learn to distinguish between free and obstructed spaces.

This work demonstrates the importance of neural networks in environmental perception and in developing robots capable of making decisions while navigating their surroundings.
